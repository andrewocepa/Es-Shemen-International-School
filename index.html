Core Features

Why families choose Es Shemen

Es Shemen International School (ESIS) delivers the A.C.E. School of Tomorrow curriculum — a Bible-based programme that shapes character while driving real academic achievement.

Individualised Learning

Each student works at their own pace on goals set to their ability, mastering every concept before moving on.

Christ-Centred Character

Biblical principles are built into daily learning, nurturing diligence, integrity and a heart to serve.

Global Academic Standard

A.C.E. graduates study at more than 1,400 universities worldwide — ESIS opens that same pathway.
